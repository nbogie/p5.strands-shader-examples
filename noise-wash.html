Noise wash

Per-surface marbling. Sample noise() at the texture coordinate and use it to tint the base colour.

This sketch layers a low-frequency wash (broad cloudy patches) over a high-frequency grain (fine speckle). The noise is sampled on the 3D object-space position, not the 2D texCoord — that avoids the visible seam at the sphere's UV wrap and the pinching at the poles, and makes the pattern continuous across faces of a box.

Using objectInputs.position anchors the pattern to the mesh, so it rotates with the object. Try varying the frequency multipliers (* 6, * 24) for different scales of marbling.
