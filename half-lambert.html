Half-Lambert

A softer alternative to pure Lambertian diffuse, popularised by Valve. Instead of max(N·L, 0) which kills brightness immediately on back-facing surfaces, use pow(N·L * 0.5 + 0.5, 2).

The *0.5 + 0.5 remaps [-1, 1] to [0, 1] so the back of the object still gets some light (just not much). The pow(..., 2) restores some falloff contrast.

Works well for stylised, illustrative scenes — looks gentler than full Lambert on rounded objects.
