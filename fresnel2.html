Fresnel 2 — mouse-controlled

Same rim-light idea as Fresnel, but with two of the three toruses driven by mouse position.

Top: mouseX controls the rim power (crispness). Drag left for a broad halo, right for a pinprick.

Middle: defaults (power = 3, full intensity) — reference.

Bottom: mouseY controls the rim intensity multiplier. Top of canvas = dark base, bottom = bright glow.

Strands exposes mouseX, mouseY, pmouseX, pmouseY as automatic float uniforms — no uniformFloat declaration needed, just reference them inside your shader.
