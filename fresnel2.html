Fresnel 2 — mouse-controlled

A Fresnel rim-light shader: surfaces are tinted brighter the more their normal is perpendicular to the view direction (i.e. at the silhouette). The basic formula is pow(1 - n.z, power), where n.z in view space is ~1 when the surface faces the camera and ~0 at the silhouette — so 1 - n.z picks out the rim, and the power sharpens it.

Two knobs are driven by mouse position on a single torus:

  • mouseX → power (rim sharpness). Move left for a broad halo, right for a tight pinprick. Range mapped to 1–11 across the canvas width.
  • mouseY → intensity multiplier (rim brightness). Top of canvas = no glow, bottom = bright. Range 0–3.

Strands exposes mouseX, mouseY, pmouseX, pmouseY as automatic float uniforms — no uniformFloat declaration needed, just reference them inside your shader.
