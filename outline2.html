Outline 2 — constant pixel width

Same inverted-hull recipe as outline 1, but with a smarter inflation: instead of scale(1.1), each vertex is pushed along its view-space normal by an amount proportional to the view-space depth.

The upcoming perspective divide (which scales screen size by 1/z) cancels the depth scaling — so the on-screen outline is the same pixel thickness on a far cube as on a close one.

Smooth-normal meshes only: box() would split into floating quads, since its faces have per-face normals that disagree at the corners.
