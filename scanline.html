Scanline (CRT-inspired)

Mimic an old TV / arcade CRT. Lit Lambertian fill, darkened by alternating horizontal lines, then green-tinted to suggest phosphor and slightly reduced in saturation.

Each pixel asks "is this row light or dark?" via step(0.5, fract(viewY * 0.5)) where viewY is the view-space y position (passed down from cameraInputs). That keeps lines roughly horizontal on screen even as the camera orbits — true screen-space scanlines would need post-process framebuffer access.

Try scrolling-zoom: closer objects get more lines crossing them, just like a CRT.

Press any key to hide.
