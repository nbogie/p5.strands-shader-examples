Hemisphere lighting

Cheap "global" lighting in one line: mix between a sky colour (above) and a ground colour (below) based on the normal's vertical component.

Formula: mix(groundColor, skyColor, n.y * 0.5 + 0.5). In p5's view space, +y points down, so we use -n.y to mean "facing up". Surfaces facing the sky get the sky tint, surfaces facing the ground get the ground tint, and slopes get an interpolated colour.

No light setup needed. Surprisingly readable for free.
