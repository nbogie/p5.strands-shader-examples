Outline 1 — applied

The minimal inverted-hull outline applied to a small scene. Each building is a hierarchy of stacked cubes — one big, two mediums, several smalls — drifting toward random target points and rotating at its own pace.

The outline is applied per cube inside a building, then internal seams are covered by the per-part lit fills, so only the building's exterior silhouette shows the outline.

Part colours are picked from a palette via a biased random function (heavier weight on the first colour), which gives each building a "dominant colour with accents" feel.
