Toon shader 1 — base 3-band

Quantized diffuse lighting on a single material slot. Reads fill() only.

For each pixel: compute N·L against a hard-coded view-space light direction, then quantize with two step() calls to one of three intensities (0.35, 0.7, 1.0). Multiply the base color by that intensity.

Limitation: shadow tone is just a dimmed version of the base color — no separate hue.
