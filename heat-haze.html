Heat haze (post-process filter)

A filter shader — applied after geometry rendering via filter(hazeShader). Each pixel of the output is sampled from the rendered canvas at a slightly displaced UV, with the displacement coming from a noise field that scrolls upward over time.

The displacement is stronger near the bottom (pow(uv.y, 1.5)) — that's where the hot sand would be — and tapers to nothing near the top. Result: silhouettes wobble as if seen through rising hot air.

Filter shaders use filterColor.canvasContent (the rendered scene as a texture) and filterColor.texCoord (the screen UV), and produce a new colour with filterColor.set.

"t"/double-click: toggle toon shader
