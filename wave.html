Wave

Classic vertex-displacement wave. In worldInputs (post model matrix, so world units), add to position.y by a sine of time + position.x × a frequency.

Five spheres are lined up along the x axis so you can watch the wave move through them. Each sphere's individual vertices wobble too, since each vertex has its own world x.

Uses worldInputs (not objectInputs) so the math is in real-world units — much easier to reason about wavelength and amplitude.
