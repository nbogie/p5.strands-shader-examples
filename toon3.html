Toon shader 3 — 4-band with highlight

Adds a fourth band — the specular highlight — using specularMaterial(). Three material slots feed the shader: shadow / base / highlight.

Three thresholds (0.25, 0.6, 0.88) produce 4 zones. Computed in two mixes: first mix(shadow, base, factorDiff) for the diffuse half, then mix(that, spec, factorSpec) to overlay the highlight only where N·L > 0.88.

Highlight threshold is the same for every object — see toon-shader 4 for per-object control.
