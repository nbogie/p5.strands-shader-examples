Toon shader 2 — separate shadow colour

Same 3 bands, but the shadow tone is its own colour, not a darker base. Reads fill() (lit) and ambientMaterial() (shadow).

Quantize N·L into a mix weight w ∈ {0, 0.5, 1.0}, then final = mix(shadowColor, baseColor, w).

If ambientMaterial() isn't set, p5 falls back to the fill colour, so the result is flat. Always call both.
