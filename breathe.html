Breathe

Push every vertex along its normal by an amount that oscillates over time. The mesh inflates and deflates rhythmically.

Done in worldInputs so the amplitude is in world units. The amount is sin(t) so it cycles between -A and +A, and is multiplied component-wise into the world-space normal.

Smooth-normal meshes only. A box() would split into floating panels because adjacent faces have different normals (same caveat as the constant-width outline in outline-shader 2).
