Jelly

Noise-driven vertex wobble. Each vertex is displaced by 3D noise() sampled at a position that drifts over time.

Three independent noise samples (with different offsets) give per-axis wobble in x, y, and z — producing an organic gelatinous motion rather than a rigid wave.

Best on smooth-normal meshes — box() would still wobble, but the per-face normals don't get re-computed, so the lighting on each face stays flat-looking even as the geometry deforms.
