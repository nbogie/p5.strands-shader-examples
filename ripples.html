Ripples (sinusoidal bump pattern)

Same idea as bumps — perturb the per-pixel normal so default lighting paints in apparent surface detail — but with a regular pattern instead of noise.

Two orthogonal sine waves: normal.x += sin(pos.y * f) tilts the surface left/right based on y, normal.z += cos(pos.x * f) tilts in/out based on x. Together they create the crosshatched ripple texture you see.

Compared to noise bumps: more predictable, more obvious. Looks like a corduroy or quilted fabric. Useful for "manufactured" surfaces (grilles, woven fabric) vs the "organic" feel of noise.
