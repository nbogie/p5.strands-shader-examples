Fresnel (rim light)

Bright glow at surfaces that face away from the camera — classic stylized rim highlight.

Simplified approximation: in p5's view space, a surface facing the camera has its normal's z component near 1, and at the silhouette it drops toward 0. So 1 - n.z is high at the rim. Raise to a power (here 3) to make the rim crisper, multiply by a colour, add to the base.

Works cleanly on smooth-normal meshes (sphere, torus). Faceted meshes (box) get a hard-edged version because each face has uniform normal.
