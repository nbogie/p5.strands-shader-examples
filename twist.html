Twist

Vertex twist around the y axis. In objectInputs, position.xz is rotated by an angle proportional to position.y — so the top of a mesh rotates a lot, the middle rotates a little, the bottom rotates the other way.

The twist amount oscillates over time via sin(t), so the shapes appear to wring back and forth.

For a visible barber-pole spiral, the geometry needs vertices at multiple y levels (high detailY on cylinder/cone). Otherwise the top and bottom just shear past each other and the surface looks flat.
