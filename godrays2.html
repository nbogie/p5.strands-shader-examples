Godrays 2 — procedural overlay (no occlusion)

A cheap stylised "sun rays" overlay drawn straight over the rendered scene. For each pixel:

  1. Compute angle and distance from a fixed sun UV (using atan(dy, dx)).
  2. sin(angle * 14 + wobble) produces 14 alternating bright/dark bands radiating outward.
  3. A noise(angle, t) term in wobble slowly fans the bands so they breathe.
  4. pow(rays, 5) sharpens — most pixels are dark, only ray centres are bright.
  5. Radial falloff so the rays fade before reaching the corners.
  6. Warm tint, added (not multiplied) on top of the base pixel.

Compared with godrays 1: this is much cheaper — no per-pixel canvas re-sampling — but it has no idea where the geometry is, so the rays slice straight through foreground shapes. Read it as a stylised flourish, not as physical light scattering.
